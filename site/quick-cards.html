Your desktop shortcuts

Each job starts with a double-click on a shortcut, so you never have to find a folder or type a command. A shortcut is just a door into a folder or a job: deleting a shortcut deletes nothing else. The cards use these:

Shortcut What it opens
Open DA DA's menu, in your newest archive
New photos The folder new photos go in
New papers The folder new papers and scans go in
Archives All your archives, to look at photos and papers
Back up archives Copies everything to the backup drive

Card 1: Add photos from a phone or camera

  1. Plug in the phone or camera (or put its memory card in the computer).
  2. Double-click New photos.
  3. Make a folder named for what happened: right-click an empty spot, New > Folder, and type a name like Beach trip or Emma's birthday. No date needed: DA adds it.
  4. Copy the photos from the phone or camera into that folder.
  5. Double-click Open DA, type 1 and press Enter.
  6. Wait. DA is done when it shows Where things stand now.

Your photos are now in the year, in a folder like 2026-06 Beach trip. Copies of photos you already had are set aside, not filed twice. Keep the photos on the phone until after the next backup (Card 6).

Before step 4, you can pick a better moment for an iPhone Live Photo (Card 1b). Between steps 4 and 5, you can delete the shots you don't want (Card 1c).

Card 1b: Change the key photo in an iPhone Live Photo

The key photo is the moment a Live Photo shows. Pick a better one (eyes open, smiling) on the iPhone, before copying the photos (Card 1).

  1. In Photos, tap the Live Photo (LIVE at the top left), then Edit.
  2. Tap Live (the round icon at the bottom).
  3. Slide the white frame along the strip to the moment you want.
  4. Tap Make Key Photo, then Done. (Edit, Revert undoes it.)

In New photos you'll see IMG_1234 (as taken, with its video) and IMG_E1234 (the new key photo). Keep both: DA files them side by side.

Card 1c: Clean up camera photos before filing

After copying into New photos and before pressing 1 (Card 1): nothing is filed yet, so it's safe.

  1. Double-click New photos and open the folder you just made.
  2. Click View, then Large icons, to see the pictures.
  3. Click a shot you don't want: blurry, a finger on the lens, taken by mistake, or one of many alike. Hold Ctrl and click to pick more.
  4. Press Delete. They go to the Recycle Bin, so you can still get them back.
  5. Carry on with Card 1, step 5.

Files with the same name go together: delete both or neither (IMG_1234.HEIC and its video IMG_1234.MOV; IMG_0574.JPG and its RAW IMG_0574.CR2).

Only in New photos, never in Archives (Card 7). Copies you already have need no deleting: DA sets them aside.

Card 2: Add papers and scans

  1. Scan the papers, or save the PDF (a bill, a statement), into New papers. Several at once is fine.
  2. Double-click Open DA, type 1 and press Enter.
  3. Wait for Where things stand now.

DA files each paper by the date printed on it, in a folder for its year and month.

Card 3: Name the events in a month

For photos already filed that sit together in one month's folder.

  1. Double-click Open DA, type L and press Enter.
  2. DA lists the months. Type the number of the month and press Enter.
  3. DA shows the photos in groups, a group for each outing or day. For each group:
    • press Enter to take the name shown,
    • or type a better name, like Grandma's 80th, and press Enter,
    • or type - and press Enter to leave that group where it is.
  4. Give two groups the same name to put them in one folder.
  5. Press Enter when DA asks to move them.

Card 3b: Date old photos

For scanned prints and other photos DA couldn't date: they wait in review, a folder at a time.

  1. Double-click Open DA, type V and press Enter. The Dates page opens in the browser.
  2. Pick a folder at the top. Click the photos that were taken together (they get a tick).
  3. The big date shows DA's best guess. Press − or + to go a month older or newer, −− or ++ for a year. Or click the date and type it, like 1987-03.
  4. Press Set. The photos show dated in green.
  5. Do the next photos. Made a mistake? Press Undo.
  6. When you're done, press File dated photos (or type 1 in DA next time).

A month is enough. If you only know the year, set it anyway: the photo waits until you know the month.

Card 4: Look at photos and papers

  1. Double-click Archives.
  2. Open the archive for the years you want: the one ending 201x holds 2010 to 2019.
  3. Open photos, then organized, then the year, then the month or event.
  4. Double-click a photo to see it. Each folder also has a _contact-sheet.pdf: the whole folder on a few pages, to find the photo you're after quickly.

Papers are in the same archive, under documents, then organized, then the year.

To look in a web browser instead: double-click Open DA, type B and press Enter.

Card 5: Find something

  1. Double-click Archives.
  2. Click in the Search box at the top right of the window.
  3. Type a word from the event or the paper: Beach, 2019, birthday, Water Company.
  4. Wait a moment for the list, then double-click what you want.

Card 6: Back up (once a week)

  1. Plug in the backup drive.
  2. Double-click Back up archives.
  3. Wait. The first time takes a long while; after that, only what changed is copied.
  4. When it says You can unplug the backup drive, press any key, then unplug it.
  5. Put the backup drive away from the computer.

If it says The backup drive isn't there, plug it in and try again. If it says anything else, don't try again: call for help.

Card 7: When something looks wrong

  1. Stop. Don't move, rename or delete anything in the archive folders yourself.
  2. Take a photo of the screen with your phone, or write down what it says.
  3. Call or text for help (the number is on the first page).

Nothing is lost. DA never deletes your photos or papers: copies are only set aside, and everything DA moves can be put back. Your helper can see your screen from their home: in Windows, click Start, type Quick Assist, and read them the code it shows.

For the helper: setting up a computer

Once per computer, with DA installed (see Getting started in the user guide):

  1. Archives: da setup <folder> for each decade, e.g. D:\archives\jane-archive-202x --years 2020-2029.
  2. Old drives: in each archive, da import <drives> --apply, most complete first, then 1 Autorun and S Split by years.
  3. No questions: in the newest archive, P Pre-run setup: switch on 11 Location folders and Bypass yes.
  4. Cameras: share one list: cameras_file: ..\cameras.json in each archive's da-archive.yaml, then da cameras --detect in each, and C Cameras to fill in owners, film cameras and their years.
  5. Backup: with the backup drive plugged in, da backup --setup E:\DA-backup (its letter). Run the Back up archives shortcut it makes once.
  6. Shortcuts: right-click, Send to > Desktop (create shortcut), and rename: the newest archive's Open DA.cmd (Open DA), its intake\photos (New photos) and intake\docs (New papers), and the folder holding the archives (Archives).
  7. Fill in My archive on the first page, print the cards, and do Card 1 and Card 3b together once.
